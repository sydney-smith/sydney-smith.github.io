The Swebilius Foundation with the Yale Comprehensive Epilepsy Center has decided to fund my investigation into the role of distributed aperiodic dynamics in driving network pathology in epilepsy. Using source-localized MEG and intracranial EEG recorded from participants with epilepsy, I will develop, apply, and evaluate and novel metrics to describe the pathological network dynamics driving their symptoms.

Thanks to my co-investigators:
- Kia Nobre
- Imran Quraishi
- Hitten Zaveri

And thank you to the Sweblius Foundation, WTI BrainWorks, and the Yale Comprehensive Epilepsy Center for supporting this work.
