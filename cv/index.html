Research Profile

  • I study how neural systems transform dynamic sensory information into adaptive behavior, with a particular focus on how this transformation is implemented by physiologically plausible patterns of neural activity within and across brain systems. To this aim, I develop and apply approaches that preserve a link between measurable neural signals and the physiological mechanisms that generate behavior.
